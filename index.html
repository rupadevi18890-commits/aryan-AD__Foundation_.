Education for Every Child

AD Foundation is a non-profit NGO dedicated to providing free tuition and free quality education to students from all backgrounds. We believe that every child deserves an opportunity to learn, grow, and build a successful future.

About AD Foundation

Our mission is to empower students through free education, experienced teachers, practical learning, science laboratories, computer education, and modern technology. We provide a friendly environment where students can learn without financial barriers.
